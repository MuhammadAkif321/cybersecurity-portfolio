# Module 1: Welcome to the Exciting World of Cybersecurity

## Module overview

This module introduced the meaning and purpose of cybersecurity, why security matters to organizations, and the responsibilities and skills involved in cybersecurity work.

## Learning topics

- The meaning and purpose of cybersecurity
- Why organizations protect their systems, networks, devices, people, and information
- Examples of cybersecurity responsibilities, including managing threats and installing preventive software
- Technical skills and transferable skills
- Collaboration and computer forensics as examples of useful professional skills
- How security supports important business functions

## Main takeaway

Cybersecurity is not only about technology. It also involves people, processes, and protecting information so an organization can continue to operate safely and reliably.
