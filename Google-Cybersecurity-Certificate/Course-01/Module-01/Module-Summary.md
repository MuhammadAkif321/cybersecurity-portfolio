# Module 1 Summary: Welcome to the Exciting World of Cybersecurity

## What I learned

This module introduced **cybersecurity**, also called security, as the practice of protecting information and the systems that store, process, and transmit it. The main goal is to help maintain confidentiality, integrity, and availability while preventing unauthorized access and criminal exploitation.

The module explained why security is important to organizations. Organizations depend on devices, networks, applications, and data to provide services and perform critical business activities. Security helps protect these assets, reduce harm, and support reliable business operations.

It also introduced examples of cybersecurity job responsibilities. Security professionals may help manage threats, install or configure preventive software, protect organizational information, and support investigations.

The module highlighted two broad groups of skills:

- **Technical skills:** Knowledge of specific tools, procedures, technologies, and policies.
- **Transferable skills:** Skills developed in other settings that can also be useful in cybersecurity, such as communication, collaboration, and problem-solving.

Collaboration is important because security work often involves people from different teams. Computer forensics is another important area: it involves examining digital evidence in a careful and systematic way.

## Why this knowledge matters

A security incident can affect more than computers. It can interrupt services, expose private information, damage trust, and create financial or legal consequences. Understanding the purpose of cybersecurity is the foundation for learning how organizations identify and manage these risks.

## Key takeaway

Cybersecurity protects people, information, and organizational operations. Strong security depends on both technical knowledge and transferable skills, including communication and teamwork.
