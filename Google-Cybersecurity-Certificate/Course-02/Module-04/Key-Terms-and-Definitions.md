# Key Terms and Definitions — Module 4

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Incident response** | An organized effort to identify a security incident, contain its impact, investigate what happened, remove the threat, and restore affected systems and services. |
| **Playbook** | A documented guide that provides instructions for carrying out an operational task. In cybersecurity, it can guide a team through responding to a particular type of incident. |

## Related concepts from the module

- **Preparation:** Getting people, processes, and tools ready before an incident occurs.
- **Detection and Analysis:** Identifying a possible incident and examining evidence to understand it.
- **Containment:** Limiting an incident and preventing it from causing further damage.
- **Eradication:** Removing the threat and its causes from affected systems.
- **Recovery:** Restoring affected systems and services to secure operation.
- **Post-Incident Activity:** Reviewing the incident, documenting lessons, and improving future responses.
- **Coordination:** Communicating and sharing information among relevant people throughout the response.

## Remember

A playbook explains **what steps should be followed**. Incident response is the broader process of **handling the security incident**. A playbook supports incident response by making actions more consistent and organized.
