# Learning Reflection: Conducting a Security Audit

In this portfolio activity, I reviewed a fictional company's internal security audit scenario. I examined the audit scope and goals, identified the assets managed by the IT department, and considered the risk assessment findings.

I completed a controls assessment by deciding whether each listed control was in place. I also reviewed selected PCI DSS, GDPR, and SOC-related best practices and recorded scenario-based Yes/No judgments.

One important lesson was that a control can exist but still be weak. For example, Botium Toys has a password policy, but its requirements are not strong enough. The company also monitors legacy systems, but it lacks a regular monitoring schedule and clear intervention procedures.

I learned to connect missing controls with possible risks. Excessive access can expose sensitive data, missing encryption can put payment information at risk, and missing backups can make recovery from ransomware or system failure difficult.

I also practised writing recommendations, including implementing least privilege, improving password management, encrypting sensitive data, introducing an IDS, maintaining an asset inventory, and establishing tested backups and disaster recovery plans.

I completed a separate five-question knowledge check on the security audit concepts and achieved **5 out of 5**.

This activity improved my understanding of how security audits help organizations identify weaknesses, evaluate safeguards, and prioritize security improvements. I plan to build on this foundation through further course activities and practical exercises.

## Skills practised

- Reviewing audit scope and objectives
- Identifying organizational assets
- Recognizing threats, risks, and vulnerabilities
- Assessing administrative, technical, and physical controls
- Reviewing compliance-related best practices
- Writing risk-based security recommendations

*This is a reflection on a simulated learning activity. It does not claim that I conducted a real-world professional audit.*
