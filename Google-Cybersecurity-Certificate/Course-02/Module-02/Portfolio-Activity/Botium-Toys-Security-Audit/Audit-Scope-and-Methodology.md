# Audit Scope and Methodology

## 1. Audit purpose

The purpose of this simulated internal security audit is to review Botium Toys' security controls and selected compliance best practices, identify gaps that may expose the organization to risk, and recommend improvements.

## 2. Scope

According to the supplied report, the audit scope is the entire security program at Botium Toys. It includes the assets managed by the IT department and the controls and compliance practices used to protect them.

## 3. Audit goals

- Review the assets managed by the IT department.
- Evaluate the controls currently in place.
- Review selected compliance best practices.
- Identify gaps that could affect customers, employees, assets, and business continuity.
- Recommend improvements to strengthen the organization's security posture.

## 4. Assets in scope

The report identifies these asset groups:

- On-premises equipment used for office business needs.
- Employee equipment, including desktops, laptops, smartphones, remote workstations, headsets, cables, keyboards, mice, docking stations, and surveillance cameras.
- Retail products stored in the adjoining warehouse and sold in-store and online.
- Systems, software, and services for accounting, telecommunications, databases, security, e-commerce, and inventory management.
- Internet access and the internal network.
- Data retention and storage.
- Legacy systems that have reached end of life and require human monitoring.

## 5. Methodology

I used the information in the fictional company's scope, goals, and risk assessment report together with the course's control categories document.

The review followed these steps:

1. Identify the audit scope, goals, and assets.
2. Review the risk assessment and its additional comments.
3. Compare the reported security practices with the control categories and purposes.
4. Mark each listed control Yes or No according to whether the scenario says it is currently in place.
5. Review the PCI DSS, GDPR, and SOC-related checklist statements against the scenario.
6. Record important gaps and prepare recommendations.

## 6. Risk context

The supplied report gives Botium Toys a risk score of **8/10**, described as fairly high. It also says the potential impact from loss of an asset is rated medium because the IT department does not know which assets would be at risk. The risk of asset loss or regulatory fines is rated high because necessary controls and compliance best practices are missing.

## 7. Limitations

This assessment relies only on the fictional scenario and supporting course documents. I did not inspect real systems, interview staff, test controls, review technical configurations, or independently verify compliance. Therefore, the results are educational, scenario-based judgments rather than a formal audit opinion.
