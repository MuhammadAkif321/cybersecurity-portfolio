## Control categories

The course groups controls into three categories:

- **Administrative/Managerial:** Policies, procedures, responsibilities, least privilege, separation of duties, and disaster recovery planning.
- **Technical:** Firewalls, IDS/IPS, antivirus, encryption, backups, and password management tools.
- **Physical/Operational:** Locks, CCTV, and fire detection/prevention systems.

Controls can also serve different purposes:

- **Preventative:** Tries to stop an incident before it happens.
- **Detective:** Helps identify an incident or suspicious activity.
- **Corrective:** Helps restore systems or data after an incident.
- **Deterrent:** Discourages unwanted activity.

## Important interpretation

A Yes answer means the scenario reports that the control exists. It does **not** automatically mean the control is effective, sufficiently configured, or compliant. For example, Botium Toys has a password policy, so the checklist answer is Yes, but the policy is weak and should be improved. Similarly, legacy systems are monitored, but the monitoring process is not sufficiently organized.

These results are based on the supplied fictional scenario and are not verified technical test results.
