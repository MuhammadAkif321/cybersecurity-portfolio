# Learning Reflection: Security Domains

In this module, I studied how cybersecurity work is organized into security domains and learned how threats, risks, and vulnerabilities can affect an organization. I was also introduced to ransomware, the three layers of the web, and the NIST Risk Management Framework.

One important concept from this module is the seven-step NIST RMF process: Prepare, Categorize, Select, Implement, Assess, Authorize, and Monitor. It provides an organized approach to managing security and privacy risks.

I completed the module quizzes, match-the-column activities, and graded assignment, and reported achieving 100% marks in each. These assessments helped me review the concepts covered in the module.

I want to continue strengthening my understanding by connecting these concepts to practical cybersecurity tools and realistic security scenarios in future modules.
